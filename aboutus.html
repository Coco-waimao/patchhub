About ChinaPatchFactory

Your trusted partner for premium custom patches. Factory direct from China to the world.

Our Story

Founded in 2009, ChinaPatchFactory began as a small embroidery workshop in KunShan, China. With a passion for quality craftsmanship and a commitment to customer satisfaction, we have grown into one of the leading custom patch manufacturers in the industry.

Over the past 15 years, we have served over 10,000 customers worldwide, ranging from small businesses and clubs to Fortune 500 companies. Our patches have adorned uniforms, fashion apparel, military gear, and promotional items across the globe.

What sets us apart is our dedication to quality, our factory-direct pricing, and our no-minimum-order policy. Whether you need 1 piece or 100,000 pieces, we treat every order with the same level of care and attention to detail.
