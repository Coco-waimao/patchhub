Frequently Asked Questions

Embossed means the design is raised from the surface of the metal, creating a three-dimensional effect. Debossed means the design is recessed or pressed into the metal. Both techniques create a tactile, premium appearance.

We offer base metals including brass, copper, and iron, with plating options such as gold, silver, nickel, black nickel, and various antique finishes. Each finish creates a different aesthetic - from bright and modern to aged and vintage.

Metal patches can be attached in several ways depending on your application: pin backing with butterfly clutches for temporary attachment, adhesive for permanent application, or holes for sewing. We can recommend the best attachment method based on how you plan to use the patch.

Metal patches are extremely durable! They won't fade, tear, or wear out like fabric patches. With proper care (avoiding harsh chemicals and excessive moisture), metal patches can last for decades and even become heirloom pieces.
