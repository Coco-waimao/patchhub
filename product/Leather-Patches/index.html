Frequently Asked Questions

Genuine leather patches are made from real animal hide and develop a beautiful patina over time. They have a unique, natural look and feel. Faux (vegan) leather patches are made from synthetic materials like PU, offering a consistent appearance at a lower price point while being cruelty-free.

Debossing is a process where a design is pressed into the leather, creating a recessed impression. Embossing creates a raised design. Both techniques create a tactile, premium appearance without using ink or thread.

Leather patches are typically sewn on for the most secure and professional appearance. We can also add adhesive backing for temporary placement before sewing. For hats and bags, rivets are another popular attachment method.

Yes! Genuine leather patches develop a beautiful patina over time with use and exposure. This natural aging process adds character and uniqueness to each patch. Faux leather patches maintain a consistent appearance and won't develop a patina.
