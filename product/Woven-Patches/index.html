Frequently Asked Questions

Woven patches are created by weaving threads together rather than stitching on top of a fabric base like embroidery. This creates a thinner, flatter patch that can reproduce much finer detail, small text, and photo-like imagery. Embroidered patches have a textured, three-dimensional look while woven patches have a smooth, flat appearance.

Woven patches can reproduce text as small as 4pt (about 1.4mm) while still remaining readable. This makes them ideal for patches that need to include small text, serial numbers, or intricate details that would be lost with embroidery.

Absolutely! Woven patches excel at reproducing complex gradients and photographic imagery. The weaving process allows for smooth color transitions and detailed imagery that embroidery cannot match. We can reproduce almost any design with exceptional clarity.

Yes, woven patches are very durable! The tight weave creates a strong, long-lasting patch that holds up well to washing and regular wear. They may be thinner than embroidered patches, but don't let that fool you - the high-density weave ensures excellent durability.
