The Short Version

What Makes Custom PVC Patches Different

A PVC patch is not stitched. Coloured liquid polyvinyl chloride is poured into a metal mould cut from your artwork, cured layer by layer, then released as one flexible solid piece.

Waterproof by nature

Non-porous soft PVC absorbs almost no water. Rain, sweat, mud and seawater rinse off instead of soaking in, which is why outdoor and marine programs default to rubber patches.

Fine detail survives

Small lettering, thin outlines and tight logo curves are moulded, not approximated by stitches. Detail that would clog an embroidery file comes out sharp in PVC.

Built for abrasion

The cured surface resists scuffing, flexing and repeated washing. Edges stay sealed, so there is no merrow border to unravel after a season of hard use.

Easy to clean

A damp cloth is enough. Unlike fabric patches, PVC does not trap dirt in thread, which matters for uniforms, tactical kit and children's backpacks.

For the full material breakdown, weather resistance numbers and care instructions, our PVC patches durability guide goes considerably deeper than a product page can.

                    Moulding Options
                    
2D vs 3D PVC Patches: Which Mould Do You Need?

                    
Both start with the same soft PVC and the same custom metal mould. The difference is how many levels the artwork is built from, and that single choice affects look, lead time and tooling cost more than any other specification.

                

                

                    

                        2D · Flat & Graphic
                        
2D PVC patches

                        
One raised level with recessed lines separating colour zones. The surface reads flat and clean, which suits corporate logos, wordmarks, simple badges and any artwork drawn as a vector. Tooling is simpler, so 2D is the economical choice for large runs and tight deadlines.

3D PVC patches

                    Beyond the Standard Mould
                    
Special Effects for Custom Rubber Patches

                    
Standard 2D and 3D moulding covers most orders. When a project needs a specific visual effect or a functional property, these finishes can be combined with either moulding style. Tell us at the proof stage so we can build it into the mould plan.

                

                

                    
Glow in the dark
Phosphorescent pigment is mixed into selected colour cavities so the patch charges under light and glows after dark. Best for safety markings, night events and kids' gear.

                    
UV printed detail
Photographic gradients, tiny QR codes and fine type are printed onto the cured PVC surface instead of being moulded. Best when the artwork has more tones than colour cavities allow.

                    
Photochromic colour change
Pigment shifts colour in direct sunlight and returns indoors. A low-cost way to add a surprise element to event and promotional patches.

                    
Thermochromic effect
Heat-reactive compound changes colour with temperature, including the warmth of a hand. Popular for interactive promotional work.

                    
Translucent or clear PVC
Transparent and semi-transparent compounds let underlying fabric or a printed layer show through. Best for subtle tone-on-tone branding on apparel.

                    
Cut-outs and die-cut shapes
The mould follows the outline of your artwork instead of a standard rectangle or circle, including open windows inside the design. Best for distinctive silhouettes.

                    
Glitter and metallic effect
Flake or pearlescent compound is blended into a colour cavity for sparkle that is sealed inside the rubber rather than sprinkled on top.

                    
Embedded NFC
A thin NFC chip can be sealed inside the mould so a tap opens a product page, event registration or authenticity record. Ask about it at the proof stage because it changes the thickness plan.

                    
Special edge treatments
Raised rims, recessed channels and two-tone borders are cut directly into the mould. A cheap way to make a simple logo look engineered.

                    Buyer's Vocabulary
                    
PVC Patches, Rubber Patches, Silicone Patches: What Buyers Actually Mean

                    
Search results mix several names for the same moulded product. Suppliers use them interchangeably, which makes comparison shopping confusing. Here is the plain-English version so you know exactly what you are ordering.

                

                

                    
Custom PVC patches
The most accurate trade name. The badge is moulded from soft polyvinyl chloride compound, coloured in separate cavities. This is the term most North American and European buyers use when they want a quote.

                    
Rubber patches / custom rubber patches
The same product described by feel rather than chemistry. Buyers searching for rubber patches usually want the flexible, waterproof, wipe-clean badge, not a vulcanised rubber part.

                    
Silicone patches / 3D silicone patches
A common marketplace name, especially on B2B platforms. Most listings described as silicone are in fact soft PVC. True silicone behaves differently and is rarely used for this price point, so confirm the compound if it matters to your program.

                    
Soft PVC patches
Emphasises the flexible grade of PVC, as opposed to rigid PVC sheet. Shore hardness for patch compound typically sits between 60A and 80A, which is why a patch bends with fabric instead of cracking.

                    
2D and 3D PVC patches
Not different materials, just different mould depths. 2D has one raised level, 3D has several sculpted levels. Both take the same backings and the same special finishes.

                    
PVC morale patches
Removable hook-and-loop badges used on tactical gear, plate carriers and backpacks. The term describes the use, not the construction, so spec the backing explicitly.

                    
PVC hat patches / PVC patches for hats
Usually 1.5 to 3 inches with a sew-on or heat-seal backing, sized to sit on a curved cap front without distorting the moulded detail.

                    
PVC logo patches
Brand marks converted into moulded colour zones with Pantone matching. The most common corporate use, and the one where our free digital proof saves the most rework.

                

                

                    
One naming trap worth avoiding

                    
In some markets "PVC stamp" refers to a clear rubber stamp for scrapbooking or a craft ink pad. That is a different product entirely. If you are sourcing moulded badges for apparel or gear, quote against the specification on this page rather than a marketplace keyword.

                    Application & Care
                    
How to Apply and Care for PVC Patches

                    
Iron-on PVC patches are convenient, but the adhesive has limits. These are the parameters we recommend for heat application, followed by the cleaning rules that keep a moulded badge looking new.

                

                

                    
~180°C / 356°FIron temperature

                    
20–25 secPressing time

                    
~3 kgFirm hand pressure

                

                

                    

                        
 Iron-on application

                        
Preheat the garment, position the patch, cover it with a thin cloth or parchment and press firmly without sliding. Do not use steam. Let it cool completely before flexing the fabric, then press the reverse side for another 10 seconds on thicker materials. For caps and curved surfaces, a heat press at about 160°C / 320°F for 15 to 20 seconds gives a more even result than a hand iron.

                    

                    

                        
 When heat will not work

                        
Heat-activated adhesive struggles on nylon, waterproof or DWR-coated shells, leather, mesh and heavily textured knits. On those materials choose sew-on or hook and loop instead. If a patch must be removable for washing, or the gear lives outdoors, hook and loop is the safer specification.

                    

                

                

                    

                        
 Cleaning and storage

                        
Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid solvents, bleach and abrasive scourers, which can dull the surface. Patches can be machine washed on a gentle cycle inside out, but high heat drying is hard on adhesive and hook-and-loop backings. Store flat, away from direct sunlight and sharp objects.

                    

                    

                        
 Field repairs

                        
If a sew-on edge lifts, restitch through the moulded groove rather than gluing over the face. If a hook sheet loses grip, brush out lint or replace the hook panel. Adhesive backings cannot be reliably re-applied once removed, which is why we treat them as temporary by design.

                    Design Readiness
                    
Artwork Requirements for Custom PVC Patches

                    
Most production problems start in the artwork, not the mould. Because every colour is a physical cavity, a file that looks perfect on screen can still be impossible to pour. Use this checklist before you send anything.

                

                

                    
Minimum line width of 0.5 mmThinner lines can fill with compound or break when the mould is cut. Hairline logos need redrawing, not scaling up.

                    
Lettering at 6 pt or largerSmall reverse text inside a coloured cavity is the most common failure. We will flag it on the free proof.

                    
Solid colours, not gradientsMoulded PVC uses discrete colour cavities. Photographic gradients need UV printing onto the cured surface instead.

                    
A maximum of 12 coloursEach colour is a separate pour. Split complex artwork into zones or let us simplify it during the proof stage.

                    
Vector files preferredAI, PDF or EPS give clean cavity edges. JPG and PNG are accepted, but we will redraw them before moulding.

                    
State the finished size and shapeSend the outline you want, including cut-outs. A square logo and a die-cut silhouette need different moulds.

                    
Name your Pantone referencesBrand-critical colours should be specified as Pantone codes, not screen values, so the compound can be matched.

                    
Tell us where it will be attachedFabric type and placement determine the backing, which in turn affects the mould plan.

                    FAQ
                    
Custom PVC Patches: Frequently Asked Questions

                    
Questions grouped by what people are actually trying to decide: whether PVC suits the job, how to prepare artwork, and how ordering works.

                

                

                    

                        
General

                        

                            What are custom PVC patches and how are they made?
                            
Custom PVC patches are soft rubber emblems made by pouring coloured liquid polyvinyl chloride into a custom metal mould, curing each layer with heat, then trimming the finished shape. Because the artwork is built from moulded layers rather than stitched thread, fine lines, small lettering and sharp logo edges stay crisp. We make a mould from your approved digital proof, so every patch in the run is identical.

                        

                        

                            Are PVC patches waterproof?
                            
Yes. Soft PVC is a non-porous plastic with a water absorption rate below 0.5 percent, so the patch itself does not soak up rain, sweat or seawater and will not fray, rot or fade the way thread can. The backing you choose still matters: sewn or hook-and-loop attachments stay fully waterproof, while iron-on and self-adhesive backings depend on the adhesive rather than the PVC.

                        

                        

                            How long do PVC patches last outdoors?
                            
A well-made PVC patch typically lasts several years of regular outdoor use. UV stabilisers slow sun damage, and the moulded surface resists abrasion, mud and repeated washing. Our durability guide covers the material science, weather testing and care steps in detail.

                        

                        

                            Are you a factory or a trading company?
                            
We are a China-based custom patch manufacturer with our own mould making, colour mixing, curing and inspection lines, not a trading intermediary. You work directly with the production team, which is why we can offer factory direct pricing, free digital proofs and no minimum order quantity. We ship worldwide via DHL and FedEx.

                        

                    

                    

                        
Design

                        

                            What is the difference between 2D and 3D PVC patches?
                            
A 2D patch has one flat raised level with recessed detail, giving a clean, graphic look at a lower tooling cost. A 3D patch uses multiple raised levels and sculpted contours, so logos get real depth and a rounded, tactile finish. Both are moulded from the same soft PVC and take the same backings. Choose 2D for flat logos and text, 3D for mascots, badges and dimensional artwork.

                        

                        

                            How many colours can I use, and can you match Pantone colours?
                            
Up to 12 colours per design. Each colour is a separate pour into its own mould cavity, so we map your artwork into colour zones during the proof stage. We match Pantone references for brand-critical work and send a digital proof showing every colour callout before production starts.

                        

                    

                    

                        
Ordering

                        

                            Is there a minimum order quantity, and how fast will I get a proof?
                            
There is no minimum order quantity. You can order a short run for a sample or a single team, or a full production batch. We send a free digital proof within 12 hours of receiving your artwork and requirements, and revisions are unlimited until you approve it.

                        

                        

                            How is pricing calculated for PVC patches wholesale?
                            
Wholesale pricing depends on size, number of colour cavities, 2D or 3D tooling complexity, backing type and total quantity. Larger runs spread the one-off mould cost across more units, so the per-piece price drops as quantity rises. Send us your design, dimensions, backing and quantity for an exact quote rather than a guessed unit price.
