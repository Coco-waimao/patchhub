Frequently Asked Questions

PVC (polyvinyl chloride) patches are more rigid and hold detail extremely well, making them perfect for sharp lines and text. Silicone patches are softer and more flexible, with a slightly different feel. Both are waterproof and durable, but PVC is more commonly used for patches due to its excellent detail reproduction and cost-effectiveness.

Yes! PVC patches are 100% waterproof. They can be completely submerged in water without any damage. This makes them perfect for outdoor gear, water sports equipment, tactical gear, and any application where exposure to moisture is expected.

PVC patches are extremely durable! They resist abrasion, temperature extremes, UV exposure, and chemicals. They won't fade, crack, or peel even with heavy use. Many of our military and tactical customers report patches lasting 5-10 years or more with regular use.

Yes! We can apply heat-activated adhesive to the back of PVC patches for iron-on application. However, due to the thickness of PVC patches, we recommend using higher heat and longer pressing time than with fabric patches. Sew-on is still the most secure attachment method for heavy-duty applications.
