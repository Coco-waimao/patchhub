Frequently Asked Questions

Chenille is a type of yarn with a fuzzy, caterpillar-like texture. When used in patches, it creates a soft, plush, three-dimensional appearance. Chenille patches have been a classic for varsity letterman jackets since the early 1900s, giving them that iconic vintage look.

Chenille patches are made by stitching loops of chenille yarn through a fabric base (usually felt or twill). The loops are then cut to create the fuzzy texture. Most chenille patches also have an embroidered outline around the chenille elements to give them definition and prevent fraying.

Chenille letters are typically made in larger sizes due to the nature of the material. The most common sizes are 4", 6", 8", and 10" for varsity letters. We can make custom sizes as well, but we generally recommend nothing smaller than 2" for chenille to show off the texture properly.
