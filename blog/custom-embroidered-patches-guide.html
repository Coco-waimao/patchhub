Custom Embroidered Patches: The Complete Guide 2026

ChinaPatchFactory Team · Custom Patch Experts

Discover everything you need to know about custom embroidered patches, from design fundamentals to production techniques. Learn why embroidered patches remain the top choice for businesses worldwide...

What Are Custom Embroidered Patches?

Custom embroidered patches are decorative pieces of fabric featuring designs stitched with thread onto a backing material. These versatile branding elements have been used for centuries to identify military units, represent organizations, and add personal flair to clothing and accessories. Embroidery is one of the oldest textile arts, with evidence dating back thousands of years to ancient civilizations across the globe (Wikipedia, 2026).

Types of Embroidered Patch Coverage

When ordering custom embroidered patches, one of the most important decisions you'll make is choosing the embroidery coverage level.

50% Embroidery Coverage

50% embroidery coverage means approximately half of the patch surface is covered with thread, leaving the background twill fabric visible in the remaining areas.

75% Embroidery Coverage

75% embroidery coverage strikes an excellent balance between visual impact and cost. With three-quarters of the surface covered in thread, these patches offer richer color saturation.

100% Embroidery Coverage

100% embroidery coverage represents the premium tier of embroidered patches, with the entire surface covered in thread for maximum visual impact.

Embroidered Patch Border Options

The border of your custom embroidered patch serves both functional and aesthetic purposes.

Merrowed Border (Overlock)

The merrowed border, also known as an overlock border, is the traditional choice for embroidered patches.

Heat-Cut Border

Heat-cut borders use a hot knife to seal the patch edges, creating a clean, flat border.

Laser-Cut Border

For the ultimate in precision and detail, laser-cut borders offer unmatched accuracy.

Backing Options for Embroidered Patches

Choosing the right backing for your custom embroidered patches is crucial for ensuring proper attachment and longevity.

Sew-On Backing

The traditional and most permanent option, sew-on backing requires stitching the patch directly onto your garment or accessory.

Iron-On Backing

Iron-on backing features a heat-activated adhesive that bonds to fabric when pressed with a hot iron.

Velcro Backing

Velcro (hook and loop) backing allows patches to be attached and removed repeatedly.

Adhesive Backing

Peel-and-stick adhesive backing offers the ultimate convenience for temporary applications.

Design Tips for Embroidered Patches

Creating effective embroidered patch designs requires understanding the medium's strengths and limitations.

Keep Text Large and Legible

Small text can become illegible when embroidered. As a general rule, letters should be at least 1/4 inch (6mm) tall for good readability.

Limit Fine Details

While modern embroidery machines are incredibly precise, extremely fine details may not translate well to thread.

Choose Colors Wisely

Thread colors can differ slightly from digital representations. Using high-contrast color combinations is recommended.

Applications and Use Cases

Custom embroidered patches serve countless purposes across industries.

Corporate Branding

Companies use embroidered patches for employee uniforms, promotional items, and corporate gifts.

Military and Tactical

Military units, police departments, fire departments, and security companies rely on embroidered patches for identification.

Fashion and Apparel

From streetwear brands to high-fashion designers, embroidered patches add value and distinction to clothing lines.
