Backing Options

Choose the perfect backing for your application

No Backing

Popular
  • Adapts to all fabrics
  • Thin thickness
  • Can be washed frequently

Method: Sewing

Iron-On Backing

Popular
  • Quick application
  • Strong adhesion
  • No sewing needed

Method: Iron or heat press

Velcro (Hook & Loop)

Popular
  • Easy attach/detach
  • Reusable
  • Great for tactical gear

Method: Sew velcro loop to garment

Plastic Backing

  • Adapts to all fabrics
  • Hard and durable
  • Not easy to deform

Method: Sewing or gluing

3M Self-Adhesive

  • Peel and stick
  • Easy application
  • Strong 3M adhesive

Method: Peel and stick

Safety Pin Backing

  • Easily removable
  • Re-usable
  • No sewing needed

Method: Pin directly to fabric
